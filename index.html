Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

This application ("the App") is operated by Takuhiko Hiramoto for the purpose of non-commercial research into landscape and travel trends. The results are used to help select subjects for original artwork.

1. Information We Collect

The App accesses the Pinterest API only to read aggregated trend data (e.g. trending keywords via the trends/keywords endpoint). The App:

  • does not collect, store, or process personal information of Pinterest users;
  • does not post, modify, or delete any content on Pinterest;
  • does not sell or share any data with third parties.

2. How We Use Information

Aggregated trend keywords are used solely to inform the selection of landscape subjects for non-commercial creative work. No user-level or personally identifiable information is involved.

3. Data Storage

Any API credentials (access tokens) used by the App are stored privately by the operator and are not shared publicly. No Pinterest user data is retained.

4. Third-Party Services

The App interacts with the Pinterest API in accordance with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and Terms of Service.
